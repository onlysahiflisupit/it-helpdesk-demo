# Customer communication sample

**Practice scenario:** a display disconnects after a laptop wakes from sleep.

## First response

Hi Casey,

Thanks for the details. I understand the display disconnects after the laptop wakes up, even though it works before sleep.

Could you confirm whether the laptop screen continues working and whether the display shows “No signal”? Please also let me know a convenient time to check the cable and input settings with you.

I will record the results in this ticket so you do not need to repeat the troubleshooting steps.

Only

## Follow-up after a hypothetical fix

Hi Casey,

The display stayed connected after three sleep-and-wake checks using the replacement HDMI cable. Please try your usual presentation setup and let me know if the issue returns.

I have recorded the cable change and the checks in the ticket. We can reopen it if the display disconnects again.

Only

These messages are writing samples. The three checks in the hypothetical example are not reported as a real incident or a test executed on hardware.
